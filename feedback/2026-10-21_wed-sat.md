## Wed 2026-10-21

- `0d53c60` Added `notes/2026-10-21.md`, covering:
  - **Startup:** `make up` starts Uvicorn and FastAPI. `DatasetStore` loads
    `/data` (`broadband_100/`). The page calls `GET /api/analysis` and then
    `/api/measurements/{id}`.
  - **Uploads:** `POST /api/datasets` stages files in the container's `/tmp`
    and runs the full analysis before making them active. A rejected upload
    keeps the previous dataset. A lock prevents reads during replacement.
    Uploads are lost when the container stops.
  - **Validation:** at most 1000 files and 100 MB; nonempty, uniquely named
    CSVs; a frequency in each filename; finite numbers; at least 8 rows and
    8 distinct fields per sweep; at least 4 resonance points for Kittel.
    Decreasing linewidth vs frequency (negative damping) is rejected. There
    is no R² acceptance threshold.
  - **Plotting:** Plotly.js only. All fits and unit conversions are done in
    Python.
  - **Observation:** the full analysis runs twice per upload (once to validate
    and once when the results are requested).
- **Not verified:** the app was not launched, and no tests or `make verify`
  were run for this change.
